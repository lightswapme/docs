---
title: "History"
description: "Every swap, deposit, withdrawal, and Earn operation in one feed."
---

Your account keeps one activity feed for everything: swaps, deposits, withdrawals, and Earn operations.

Each entry shows the type, the amount, and the status. Open an entry for the details, including transaction hashes.

Two habits make this page pay off:

- Check History before contacting support about an account operation. The entry usually shows what stage it's on.
- Copy the transaction hash from the entry when you do write to support. It's the fastest way for us to trace a transfer.
