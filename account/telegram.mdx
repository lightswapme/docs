LightSwap runs as a mini app inside Telegram: [t.me/LightSwapAppBot/lightswap](https://t.me/LightSwapAppBot/lightswap).

## What you can do there

- Swap crypto with the same four-step flow as the website.
- Track swaps as they move from deposit to payout.
- Share a Telegram referral link and earn the same referral rewards.

The swap flow itself works the same way as on the website. If you know [how swaps work](/how-swaps-work), you know the mini app.

## Telegram referral codes

The mini app has its own referral codes, starting with `tg_`. Share your link from the app's referral section. It opens the mini app directly.

The terms are the same as website referrals: **0.25% of referred swap volume in USDC**, claimable from **10 USDC**. The full rules are on the [Referrals](/account/referrals) page.

## Support in Telegram

For swap help, message [@LightSwapSupportBot](https://t.me/LightSwapSupportBot). Include your order ID and transaction hash, same as anywhere else.
