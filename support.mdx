Support is available around the clock. A complete first message gets the fastest answer.

## Problem with a swap?

Include these in your first message:

- Your order ID, from the swap page
- The transaction hash of your deposit
- The asset and network you sent
- The address where you expected the payout
- One sentence about what happened

## Integrating as a partner?

For API or widget questions, include the endpoint you're calling, the full error response, and a timestamp. Never send your API key, and support will never ask for it.

<Note>
  LightSwap support never asks for your seed phrase, private keys, or API keys. Anyone who does isn't us.
</Note>
