title: "Fees and rates"
description: "What a LightSwap quote includes, which costs stay separate, and why the final amount can differ from the estimate."
---

All pricing is shown before you send anything.

## The quote is the price

The quote shows the rate and the amount you'll receive. That number includes the service fee and the network fees on our side. Nothing is added afterwards.

The only separate cost is the network fee your own wallet charges to send the deposit. Your wallet shows it before you confirm the transfer.

| Cost | What it is | Where you see it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service fee | Executing the swap, including routing and network costs on our side | Built into the quoted rate |
| Network fee on your deposit | What the blockchain charges to move your coins to the deposit address | Your wallet, before you confirm |

## Why the final amount can differ

Quotes float with the market. Between the quote and the deposit confirmation, prices can move, so the final amount can come out slightly higher or lower. The difference is usually a fraction of a percent.

## Minimums and maximums

Every pair has a minimum and a maximum. They depend on the assets, the networks, and the route available at that moment. The swap form shows the allowed range before you continue.

<Warning>
  Send the exact amount from your quote. Sending less than the minimum, or a different amount than quoted, can pause the swap for manual review.
</Warning>

## Quotes expire

A quote is valid for a limited time, shown on the swap page. If it expires before your deposit arrives, don't send to the old details. Start a new swap to get a fresh rate and new deposit instructions.
