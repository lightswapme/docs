**LightSwap** is an instant crypto exchange. Swap Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoins, and 1,000+ other assets across dozens of networks. No account, no KYC, and no one holds your funds.

A swap works like this:

1. Pick the pair and the amount.
2. Review the rate and the amount you'll receive.
3. Send your coins to the deposit address we give you.
4. The new coins arrive in your wallet.

Most swaps finish in minutes. LightSwap is non-custodial: coins move from your wallet to your wallet, never through an account we hold.
