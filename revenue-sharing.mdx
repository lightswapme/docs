**LightSwap** revenue sharing lets crypto apps, wallets, Web3 platforms, and websites earn from swap activity connected to their integration.

Activation is self-serve: no contact, no agreement, no waiting. You get an API key and a widget key instantly and earn a flat `0.5%` of the swap volume completed through them.

## Activate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to LightSwap">
    Open [lightswap.me](https://lightswap.me/account) and sign in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Account → Business">
    Select **Activate business account**. Your API key and widget key are created instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your API key">
    The API key is shown only once. Copy it and store it somewhere safe. The widget URL and embed code stay copyable from the tab at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Activating again never creates a second account and never shows the API key again. You can regenerate either key from the tab; the old key stops working immediately.
</Note>

## What you earn

You earn `0.5%` of every completed swap made through your API key or widget key. The fee is paid by LightSwap out of swap volume in USDC — it does not change the rate your users get.

**Example**:

1. Users swap `$10,000` in total through your integration.
2. Your `0.5%` share equals `$50` in USDC.

The Business tab shows your attributed volume, swap count, and earned fees as they accrue, with per-swap fee history and payout history.

## Payouts

- Claim once your available balance reaches `$10` USDC.
- Claiming credits the fees to your LightSwap account balance in USDC.
- From there, withdraw to any external wallet with the normal account withdrawal flow.

Individual users can also share a referral link from **Account → Referrals** and earn from swaps made through that link. The current referral rate is shown in the account.

## Custom agreements

Self-serve activation always uses the flat `0.5%` rate. For custom commercial terms, contact the LightSwap team.
